# Keyword research — Wise Fee Calculator

**Primary:** `wise fee calculator`
**Intent:** Tool — "how much does Wise charge to send X." High global demand (freelancer
corridors: India, Philippines, Pakistan, etc.). `wise fees` anchors 5,000–12,000/mo (US est.).
**Competition:** Low–Medium — page 1 is indie calculators (payfeecalculator, transferfees,
feescalculator) + Wise's own pages, NOT an authority finance wall. Winnable.
**Data note:** volumes are estimates (paid SEO tools gated); difficulty is SERP-observed (reliable).

## SERP observations (2026-06)
- Indie fee-calculator field + Wise/Exiap brand pages. Strong per-corridor long-tail
  ("wise fees usd to inr", "wise gbp to eur fee") — our route dropdown targets these directly.
- The differentiator Wise itself leans on: mid-market rate, no FX markup — we state this clearly.

## Our differentiation
- Per-route fee (% + fixed) for 11 corridors; effective rate; honest "fee only, rate is live" model.
- Cited from Wise's own pricing/API; dated. Reverse-friendly (fee is predictable, add on top).
