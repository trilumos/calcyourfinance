# Keyword research — PayPal Fee Calculator

**Primary:** `paypal fee calculator`
**Intent:** Tool. Dominant use case: "I'm receiving money via Goods & Services — what do I keep?"
**Competition:** Medium — established calculators exist (paypal.com has none official; third-party tools rank). High search volume; long-tail is wide open.

## SERP observations (2026-06)
- Mix of fee calculators and "PayPal fees explained" articles.
- Confusion in SERP between transaction types (G&S vs Checkout vs Friends & Family) → **content opportunity**: a clear transaction-type selector + FAQ resolves real user confusion.
- Micropayments rate (4.99% + $0.09) rarely handled by competitors.

## Our differentiation
- Transaction-type selector: Goods & Services (2.99%), Checkout (3.49%), Micropayments (4.99%).
- US + UK rates cited; cross-border surcharge toggle.
- Reverse "charge to keep $X" mode.
