# Keyword research — Stripe Fee Calculator

**Primary:** `stripe fee calculator`
**Intent:** Tool (people want to compute a fee, not read an article). Confirmed via SERP.
**Competition:** Medium — several dedicated single-tool sites rank (fee-calc.com, dodopayments, globalfeecalculator, saasadviser, checkoutpage). No DR-90 finance aggregator owns this; it's winnable.

## SERP observations (2026-06)
- Top results are purpose-built calculators + "Stripe fees explained" blog posts (merchantinsiders, acodei, flexprice).
- Most competitors are **US-only** or single-rate. Few handle multiple countries, international-card surcharge, or the reverse "charge to receive X" mode → **our wedge**.
- "Stripe Fees by Country" content exists (globalfeecalculator) → confirms country-level demand.

## Our differentiation
- 8 countries with cited per-country rates + verified date.
- International-card + currency-conversion toggles.
- Reverse mode (gross-up to a target payout).
