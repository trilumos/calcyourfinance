# Mercari Fee Calculator — keyword notes

Primary: `mercari fee calculator`

## Intent signal
US-first tool intent. Searchers are mostly Mercari US sellers wanting to know what they keep after the 10% selling fee. Secondary JP intent (Mercari Japan is large; "mercari fee calculator japan" is a real query).

## Key angles to cover
- 10% seller fee (since Jan 6, 2025 — reinstated after 2024 zero-fee experiment)
- 3.6% Buyer Protection fee (paid by buyer, not seller)
- Clarity on the 2024 fee change (many stale sources still show old model)
- Japan: same 10% seller fee
- Comparison queries: mercari vs poshmark fees, mercari vs ebay fees

## Competition level
Medium — Mercari is a top-5 US resale marketplace. "mercari fee calculator" has meaningful volume; longtails around the fee change have lower competition.
