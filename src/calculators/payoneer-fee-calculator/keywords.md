**Primary:** `payoneer fee calculator`
**Intent:** Tool — "what does Payoneer take when I get paid / withdraw." High freelancer demand
(India, Pakistan, Bangladesh, Philippines, Nigeria). `payoneer fees` anchors 6,000–15,000/mo (US est.).
**Competition:** Low — page 1 is indie calculators (feescalculator.info, tipalti, a2zcalculators,
payfeecalculator), not an authority wall. Winnable.
**Data note:** volumes are estimates (paid SEO tools gated); difficulty is SERP-observed (reliable).

## SERP observations (2026-06)
- Indie fee tools + Payoneer's own pages + Wise/NerdWallet comparison articles.
- Strong "withdrawal fee" and "fees for freelancers" intent; confusion about the method-dependent
  receiving fee (card 3.99% vs ACH/bank 1% vs marketplace free) — our method selector answers it.
