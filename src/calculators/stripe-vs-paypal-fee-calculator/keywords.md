# Keyword research — Stripe vs PayPal Fee Calculator

**Primary:** `stripe vs paypal fees`
**Intent:** Mixed comparison/tool — searchers want a verdict ("which is cheaper") AND to run their
own numbers. A calculator beats an article because the honest answer is "it depends on amount,
country and PayPal product." Confirmed via SERP.
**Competition:** Medium — lots of "Stripe vs PayPal" blog/listicle content (merchant blogs, SaaS
review sites), but very few interactive side-by-side fee calculators. The comparison-tool angle is
the wedge; blog posts can't answer the per-amount crossover.

## SERP observations (2026-06)
- Top results are comparison articles (which is better, pros/cons, features) and a few fee tables.
- Almost no purpose-built "enter an amount → see both fees side by side → winner" tools.
- Strong People-Also-Ask cluster around "is stripe cheaper than paypal", "stripe vs paypal fees on
  $100", "which is better for small business / freelancers".

## Our differentiation
- True side-by-side: both processors computed on the same sale, with a winner banner + the exact gap.
- Picks the right PayPal product (Checkout / Goods & Services / Micropayments) so the verdict is fair.
- 22 countries with cited per-country rates; international + currency-conversion surcharges on both.
- Reverse mode: which platform needs the smaller charge to hit a target take-home.
- Surfaces the effective-rate crossover where PayPal micropayments beats Stripe on tiny sales.
