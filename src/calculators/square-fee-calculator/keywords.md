# Keyword research — Square Fee Calculator

**Primary:** `square fee calculator`
**Intent:** Tool — people want to compute what Square takes on a sale. Confirmed via SERP.
**Competition:** Medium — a few dedicated single-tool sites + "Square fees explained" blogs. Most
are US-only and don't separate online / in-person / keyed rates or handle Square's other markets.

## SERP observations (2026-06)
- Square's own pricing page ranks for branded terms; third-party calculators target the long tail.
- Strong confusion in PAA about Square's *different rates per payment method* (online vs in-person
  vs keyed) — our payment-type selector answers this directly = the wedge.
- "Square fees on $X" and "how much does Square charge" are common informational/tool queries.

## Our differentiation
- 8 countries (US, CA, AU, JP, GB, IE, FR, ES) with cited per-country rates + verified date.
- Online / in-person / keyed rate types in one tool (most competitors only show one).
- Foreign-card surcharge + Irish VAT-on-fee modelled; reverse "charge to receive X" mode.
