# Keyword research — Wise vs PayPal Fee Calculator

**Primary:** `wise vs paypal fees`
**Intent:** Comparison/tool — cheapest way to send money abroad. Very high global demand.
**Competition:** Head terms ("wise vs paypal", "paypal vs wise", 8–40k/mo global) are owned by
Wise.com's own geo-targeted "vs PayPal" pages + a mature affiliate wall (Monito, Finder, Exiap,
MoneyTransfers) — **unwinnable for a new domain.** BUT smaller fee sites (InvoiceMonk, Fee-Calculator)
already surface on the **fees-qualified / international-transfer long-tail** — a calculator's lane.
**Strategy:** aim H1/SSR at the FEES angle; treat "wise vs paypal" as a bonus. High CPC.
**Data note:** volumes estimated; difficulty SERP-observed (reliable).

## Differentiation (the one thing the listicles lack)
- An interactive amount + route calculator with a live TOTAL-COST number: Wise's mid-market fee vs
  PayPal's $4.99-capped fee + ~4% hidden FX markup. Honest that PayPal wins on tiny transfers (<~$60).
