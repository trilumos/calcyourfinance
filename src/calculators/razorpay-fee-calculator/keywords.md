# Keyword research — Razorpay Fee Calculator

**Primary:** `razorpay fee calculator`
**Intent:** Tool — compute Razorpay's cut + settlement. High demand (≈90% India).
**Competition:** LOW–MED on the calculator term — page 1 is small tool sites (onlinetooler,
fusionfocus, imakash, buildmvpfast), razorpay.com barely present. Head terms ("razorpay charges",
"razorpay fees", 8–40k/mo) are a brand+aggregator wall, but the *calculator* slice (~2–5k/mo) is winnable.
**Data note:** volumes estimated (paid tools gated); difficulty SERP-observed (reliable). Low CPC (India fintech).

## Differentiation
- 2% platform fee + 18% GST modelled (effective ~2.36%); per-method (domestic/corporate/intl); reverse mode.
- Honest "UPI is NOT free on Razorpay" (platform fee applies) — a real, citable distinction vs Paytm.
